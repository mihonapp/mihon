AFRAGA Created By AFRAZ 
THE MANGA DISCOVERY & READING EXPERIENCE
============================================================

Welcome to Afraga, your vivid, personal space to discover, organize, and read
manga. Explore series through sources you choose, build a library that feels
like yours, and move between Home, Profile, Search, and Browse from one sleek
floating dock. A deep night palette, electric color, cover-led layouts, and
quick category controls make your collection feel fresh and easy to navigate.

Afraga is a reader and organizer. It does not host, bundle, or provide manga
chapters itself.


WHAT YOU CAN DO
============================================================

DISCOVER AND READ
  * Search manga from the sources/extensions you have installed and enabled.
  * Open a title, browse its available chapters, and read with the app's reader.
  * Add titles to your library so they are easy to find again.
  * Use the existing reader options, reading directions, and viewing modes.

MAKE YOUR LIBRARY YOURS
  * View your saved manga in a redesigned dark library screen.
  * Use category chips to switch between All and your library categories.
  * See cover-focused highlights and recently added or updated titles when data
    is available in your library.
  * Use the filter control to open the app's library filter and sort options.
  * Use the overflow menu for library actions such as refreshing or updating.

FOUR QUICK DESTINATIONS
  * HOME — your library and discovery-style entry point.
  * PROFILE — local reading/library highlights and profile customization.
  * SEARCH — search across installed, enabled source extensions.
  * BROWSE — find and manage available sources/extensions.

PERSONALIZE YOUR PROFILE
  * Choose a profile picture and banner image from your device's photo picker.
  * Your chosen image references and profile text are saved locally on your
    device, subject to Android's photo access permissions.
  * Review library-based counts and manga covers from your own collection.

KEEP YOUR COLLECTION ORGANIZED
  * Create and use library categories.
  * Schedule library updates and keep track of reading progress.
  * Back up and restore app data using the app's backup options.
  * Connect supported tracking services from the app's tracking settings.

The exact manga, chapter, category, and source results depend on your library,
installed extensions, network access, and the services you configure. This
build does not include sample manga or promise social feeds, reviews, or
tracking statuses beyond the features available in the app.


DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL ON ANDROID
============================================================

The debug APK files built from this project are:

  app-arm64-v8a-debug.apk  — recommended for most modern Android phones.
  app-universal-debug.apk  — broader device compatibility; a larger download.

Installation steps:

  1. Download the APK to your Android phone. You can copy it over USB or use a
     trusted file transfer method.
  2. Open Downloads or your Files app and tap the APK.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown
     apps, then return to the APK and tap Install.
  4. Open Afraga after installation and grant only the permissions you want to
     use, such as notifications or photo selection.

This project retains Mihon's Android application identity and its debug APK is
debug-signed. Android may refuse to install it over an official Mihon release
because the signing keys differ. Back up your library and app data before
changing installations. Removing an existing app can remove its local data.


FIRST STEPS IN AFRAGA
============================================================

  1. Open Browse and review the source extensions available to you.
  2. Install a source you trust, then enable/configure it as needed. Extension
     catalogs and installation steps may vary by app version and region.
  3. Open Search, choose or enable the source(s) you want to search, and enter
     a title. Search results come from those sources; they are not a built-in
     universal catalog.
  4. Open a result and select a chapter to start reading. Add the title to your
     library from its title screen to keep it in Home/Library.
  5. Return to Home to browse saved titles and use the category chips and filter
     button to narrow or sort your library.
  6. Open Profile to set a picture, banner, display name, and bio.

ABOUT EXTENSIONS AND CONTENT
============================================================

Extensions connect the app to manga sources. Afraga does not operate those
sources, control their availability, or host their content. Only install
extensions from repositories and publishers you trust. Source catalogs,
compatibility, and availability can change over time. Some sources may require
additional setup or may not work in every location.

Use extensions and content in accordance with the laws and terms that apply to
you. The project is an independent reader and is not affiliated with manga
publishers or third-party content providers.
